Questions, answered

Everything you'd want to know about sizes, pricing, access, and getting started — no digging required.

Do you offer month-to-month rentals?+

Yes. All units are rented month-to-month, so you're never locked into a long-term contract. Long-term rentals are also available if you prefer.

Are all storage units ground level?+

Yes. Every unit is ground level, making loading and unloading quick and easy without stairs or elevators.

Is there drive-up access?+

Yes. Every unit offers convenient drive-up access, so you can pull your vehicle right up to your unit door.

How secure is the facility?+

The facility features secure gated access with keypad entry and on-site security cameras to help protect your belongings.

What size storage unit do I need?+

It depends on what you're storing:

  • 5×10 – Small apartment items or extra closet storage
  • 10×10 – One-bedroom apartment or garage overflow
  • 10×20 – Two-bedroom home or full garage storage
  • 10×25 – Three-bedroom home or large garage space
  • 10×30 – Four-bedroom home or one-and-a-half-car garage

Still unsure? Contact us and we'll help you choose the right size.

How much does storage cost per month?+

Current rates run from $75/month for a 5×10 up to $200/month for a 10×30. Visit our Units & Pricing page for all sizes and unit photos.

Are there any long-term commitments or hidden fees?+

No. We offer transparent flat monthly pricing with no long-term commitments and no hidden fees.

What can I store in a unit?+

Household items, furniture, business inventory, seasonal items, vehicles, and more. For safety reasons, hazardous or illegal items are not permitted.

When can I access my storage unit?+

Gate access is available daily with your secure keypad code: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM April through September, and 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM October through March.

How do I reserve a storage unit?+

Visit our Units & Pricing page, tap your unit on the facility map, and hit Check Availability — or call (801) 350-0105 / text (385) 233-1520 and we'll set you up.

Still have a question?

Reserve online, or reach out and we'll help you find the right fit.
